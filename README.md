# About Me 👋

Sara Marques is a game designer, programmer and writer.

At Game Dev Técnico, Instituto Superior Técnico's game development association, she collaborated with dozens of devs in over 30 game jams as a game designer and programmer, using various tools and platforms, such as Unity, Unreal Engine and Godot. Through this she earned experience in game design, human machine interfaces, and visual/audio feedback.

She earned her bachelor's degree in computer science from Faculdade de Ciências da Universidade de Lisboa, where she learned the fundamentals of software development and learned Java, Python, HTML, Javascript, and had contact with many other programming languages and aspects of software engineering.

She also holds a master's degree in computer science from Instituto Superior Técnico, where she learned the fundamentals and advanced topics of game development, prototyping, and user experience. She has also worked as a monitor for Verão na ULisboa, a summer program that teaches high school students how to create their own games using Gamemaker Studio 2. 

In her professional career she did frontend development for Collab’s OneAgentWeb suite of web applications, primarily working with HTML, JS and CSS.
She overhauled the web content so it was Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) Level A compliant, rewriting the entire project to be keyboard-navigable, using proper HTML syntax and ARIA tools and replacing non-accessible third-party plugins with my own implementations. Also worked with Vue, CSHTML, Jest, CKEditor and the Salesforce API.

She is passionate, creative, and adaptable, with strong technical, communication, and problem-solving skills. She aims to pursue a career where she can apply her creativity and continue to challenge herself.
